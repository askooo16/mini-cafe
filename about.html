About Campus Café

A small coffee cart that grew into a student hangout.

Our story

Campus Café began as a small coffee cart outside the library. Students kept asking for a place to sit, charge their laptops and talk. Today we have a full counter in the Student Union and a team of student baristas.

We buy beans from local roasters and share our profits with the student scholarship fund.

What we believe

  • Good coffee should be affordable for students.
  • Everyone is welcome to stay and study.
  • Bring your own cup and save ₱10.
